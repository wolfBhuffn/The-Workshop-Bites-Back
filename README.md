# Ranger & Roost: The Workshop Bites Back

A claymation slapstick web series universe created by **wolfXvXsabyter**[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).

- **Live Site (GitHub Pages):** https://wolfbhuffn.github.io/The-Workshop-Bites-Back/
- **Repository:** https://github.com/wolfBhuffn/The-Workshop-Bites-Back.git

## What's Inside

- **Series Hub & Episodes:** Overview of *The Workshop Bites Back*, episode cards, 4-beat slapstick scripts, and AI claymation prompts[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
- **Characters & Desk Companions:** Profiles for **Ranger** (sheep in a wolf-pelt hood & boots), **Roost** (wolf in a sheep fleece suit), **wolfXvXsabyter**, and workshop sidekicks (**Pip**, **Nib**, and **Clod**)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
- **Choose Your Side & Workshop Crate:** Faction allegiance selection (**Team Ranger** with `BLEATWOLF15` and **Team Roost** with `FLUFFYFANG15`), category filters, and a slide-out crate drawer[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
- **Responsive Navigation:** Single-line desktop navigation bar with a collapsible hamburger menu on smaller screens[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
